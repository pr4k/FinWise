# Subscription logos

These files are served locally so subscription cards work without a third-party logo request. They identify matching payment names; all other names show a first-letter tile.

- `netflix`, `spotify`, `youtube`, `youtubemusic`, `applemusic`, `appletv`, `icloud`, and `audible`: [Simple Icons v16](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/v16.0.0/icons).
- `canva` and `openai`: [Simple Icons v15](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/v15.0.0/icons).
- `backblaze` and `vultr`: [Simple Icons source icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons).
- `amazon`, `primevideo`, and `disneyplus`: [Wikimedia Commons Amazon icon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amazon_icon.svg), [Prime Video icon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amazon_Prime_Video_logo_(2024).svg), and [Disney+ logo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Disney%2B_2024.svg).
- `azure`: [Wikimedia Commons Microsoft Azure icon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Microsoft_Azure.svg).

Simple Icons files have been colored to match their brands. The source artwork and brand marks remain subject to their respective source terms and trademark rules.
